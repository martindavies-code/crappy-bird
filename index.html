CODEX OF DUNSHELM

Knowledge recorded by the scholars of Treguard.

The Helmet of Justice

A magical iron helm forged with curved horns. It completely blinds the physical sight of the dungeoneer, forcing them to rely on spiritual guidance, team counsel, and arcane instinct to fly through treacherous corridors.

The Life Force Clock

Every living soul in Dunshelm is tethered to a magical Life Force Clock. It manifests as a face in an armored helmet that degrades over time:

  • Green Visor (100%–65%): Pristine vitality and high resilience.
  • Amber Guard (64%–33%): Warning stage! Helmet visor strips away, caution bells chime.
  • Red Menace (32%–1%): Critical emergency! Flesh peels, exposed jaw, pounding heartbeat.
  • Skull of Doom (0%): Death knell. Only bone remains unless resurrected!

Arcane Spells

  • D-I-S-M-I-S-S [E]: Unleashes a burst of explosive energy that shatters the nearest portcullis or pendulum blade ahead into rubble.
  • E-Y-E-S-H-I-E-L-D: Dilates time itself, slowing the world by 60% for 4 seconds for surgical flight maneuvers.
  • A-N-V-I-L: Summons an impenetrable golden armor shield, ignoring all impacts for 3.5 seconds.
  • L-E-V-I-T-A-T-E: Suspends the dungeoneer in balanced hover, bypassing gravity entirely.
